# Alarm in a Meadow

An interactive alarm-clock prototype. Turn the glass wheel to move through a day in the meadow. Time is written out in words, and the sky and shadows change as you turn the wheel.

[Open the prototype](https://emguseii.github.io/meadow-alarm/)

- Drag the wheel with a mouse or a finger. A full turn advances or rewinds twelve hours.
- Press **Enter** or **Space** to start the showreel, pause it, or resume. Press **Esc** to return to manual control.
- Focus the wheel and use the arrow keys for one-minute adjustments.

For local use, open `index.html` in a browser. The landscape JPEGs, numeral SVGs, and trimmed audio clips are included. The page needs no build step.

The wheel sounds are synthesized with Web Audio. The showreel mixes the included percussion, countryside ambience, bird song, vibration, and alarm recordings. Use the speaker icon to mute or unmute. The percussion track is [Congas and Woodblock by Adinkra Audio](https://pixabay.com/sound-effects/musical-congas-and-woodblock-526722/).
